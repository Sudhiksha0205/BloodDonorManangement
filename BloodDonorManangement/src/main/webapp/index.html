About Red Pulse

Red Pulse is a Blood Donor Management System developed to connect blood donors, patients and hospitals through one easy-to-use platform. Our mission is to reduce the time required to find blood during emergencies and encourage voluntary blood donation.

"The gift of blood is the gift of life."
